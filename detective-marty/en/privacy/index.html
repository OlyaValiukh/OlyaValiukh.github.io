Detective Marty Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Detective Marty is a single-player detective game. We don't ask you to sign up and never ask for your name. Some cases can be bought, and when hints run out you can watch an ad for one more. This page explains in plain words what data the game uses and how to control it.

In short

  • No accounts, names, contacts or location.
  • The game itself sends only anonymous daily-case answer counts and technical crash reports — no tracking.
  • Google AdMob ads appear only when you tap "One more hint — watch an ad" yourself (see Ads).
  • Control it in Profile → Privacy.

Stored only on your phone

This data is never sent anywhere and is gone if you delete the game:

  • game language, Marty's look and traits;
  • case progress: clues found, deductions, stars;
  • your daily-case answers and your streak;
  • purchased cases (restore them with "Restore purchases").

What the game sends

If sharing is on (it is by default), the game sends two kinds of data:

  1. Anonymous daily-case counts — for each daily case, only totals: how many detectives answered, how many solved it, how many chose each of the three options. One game install counts at most one answer per case. No time, no chosen proof and no identifier of a person or phone. Why: to show you how other detectives did after you answer.
  2. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) — a technical description of the error, device model, system and game versions, game language, the case where the error happened, and a random installation ID that isn't linked to you and disappears if you delete the game. Why: to find and fix bugs.

Like any internet connection, your device sends its IP address when it contacts Google's servers. We don't store or use it.

The game receives other detectives' percentages whether or not sharing is on: reading totals reveals nothing about you.

Ads

There are no banners and no ads that appear on their own. An ad is shown only after you've used three hints in a case and tap "One more hint — watch an ad" yourself. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited).

  • What the ad network receives: IP address (and from it an approximate location, country or city level), device model and system version,

language, data about ad views and taps, and on Android the phone's advertising ID. On iPhone the game does not ask for tracking permission and doesn't share the advertising identifier (IDFA).

  • Why: to show ads, count views and prevent fraud; where you consented — also to personalise ads.
  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears before the first ad.

Change your choice: Profile → Privacy → Ad settings.

  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete it in phone settings (Google → Ads).
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.
  • How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

We don't receive any data about you from Google — only total views and revenue.

Purchases

Paid cases are bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google process payments under their own rules; the game never sees your payment details and doesn't send purchase data anywhere.

Where data is stored

Game data is processed on our behalf by Google Firebase (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited) under the Firebase Data Processing Terms.

  • Daily-case counts — in a Cloud Firestore database in the European Union (Warsaw, Poland).

They aren't linked to any person, so there is no "your" data among them to delete.

  • Crash reports — on Google servers, including in the USA. Crashlytics keeps them for 90 days, then deletes them.

Ad data is processed by Google AdMob under the Google Privacy Policy. We don't sell data or share it with anyone beyond what is described above.

How to control it

  • Stats and crash reports: Profile → Privacy → turn off "Send anonymous stats and crash reports".

The game immediately stops sending answers and reports, and unsent reports are deleted from the phone. Other detectives' percentages are still shown.

  • Ads: just don't tap "One more hint — watch an ad" — then the game never contacts the ad network.

Consent: same place → Ad settings (in the EU / UK / Switzerland); advertising ID — in phone settings.

Children

The game isn't specifically aimed at children. We don't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Sharing

The "Share" button opens your phone's system share menu. The game doesn't see or store what you send or where.

Changes

If anything changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
