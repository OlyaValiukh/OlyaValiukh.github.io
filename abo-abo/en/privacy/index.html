Abo Abo Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Abo Abo is a party game played on one phone. You don't sign up and we don't ask for names. The game is free and supported by ads; you can remove ads with a one-time purchase. This page explains, in plain words, what data the game uses and how you control it.

In short

  • No accounts, names, contacts or location collected by the game.
  • The game itself sends only anonymous totals ("this dilemma was shown 1,234 times") and technical crash reports, with no tracking.
  • The game shows Google AdMob ads. The ad network receives technical data from your phone to show and count ads (see "Ads").
  • You're in control: stats via ⓘ "How to play" → "Privacy"; ads via the consent form (in the EEA / UK / Switzerland),

your phone's advertising ID settings, or the "Remove ads" purchase.

Stored only on your phone

This never leaves your phone and is gone if you delete the game:

  • your game language and whether you've seen the rules;
  • your favorite dilemmas (☆);
  • packs unlocked with an ad, and your purchases (purchases can be brought back with "Restore purchases");
  • which dilemmas this phone has already voted on, so replaying doesn't inflate the world stats;
  • game events that haven't been sent yet (for example, while offline).

What the game itself sends

If sharing is on (the default), the game sends three kinds of data:

  1. Anonymous totals per dilemma and pack: how many times a dilemma was shown, answered "A" or "B", skipped, starred or unstarred, or shared; how many times a pack was opened, offered for unlocking with an ad, unlocked with an ad, or played without one. These are kept separately per game language (Ukrainian / English). Only totals are stored, with no times, no order of events, and no identifier of a person, phone or session. Why: to remove dilemmas that don't work and add more of what people enjoy.
  2. Anonymous votes for the world percentages: for each dilemma, only the number of votes for "A" and for "B". Each phone counts at most once per dilemma. Why: so that after you choose, you can see how groups around the world chose.
  3. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): a technical description of the error, device model, OS and game versions, game language, and a random installation identifier. The identifier is created when the game is installed, isn't linked to you, and is gone if you delete the game. Why: to find and fix bugs.

As with any internet connection, your device sends its IP address when it contacts Google's servers. We don't store or use it.

The game receives the world percentages whether or not sharing is on. That's only reading totals and reveals nothing about you.

Ads

The game shows a small banner at the bottom of the screen and, only when you tap "Watch ad" yourself, an ad that unlocks a pack. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited).

  • What the ad network receives: your IP address (and from it, an approximate location at country or city level), device model and OS version,

language, data about ad views and taps, and on Android your phone's advertising ID. On iPhone the game does not ask for permission to track and does not share the advertising identifier (IDFA).

  • Why: to show ads, limit repeats, count views and prevent fraud; where you've consented, also to show ads based on your interests.
  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears before the first ad.

To change your choice: ⓘ "How to play" → "Privacy" → "Ad settings".

  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete it in your phone's settings (Google → Ads).
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.
  • How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

We don't receive any data about you from Google, only overall totals of ad views and revenue.

Purchases

"Remove ads" (and paid packs, if any are added) are paid for through the App Store or Google Play. Payments are processed by Apple and Google under their own terms; the game never sees your payment details and doesn't send purchase data anywhere. After you buy "Remove ads", the game no longer loads ads or contacts the ad network.

Where the data is kept

The game's own data is processed on our behalf by Google Firebase (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited) under the Firebase Data Processing Terms.

  • Totals and votes are stored in Cloud Firestore in the European Union (Warsaw, Poland).
  • Crash reports are stored on Google servers, including in the US. Crashlytics keeps them for 90 days and then deletes them.
  • Totals and votes are kept for as long as the game exists. They aren't linked to anyone, so there is no "your data" in them to delete.

Ad data is processed by Google AdMob under the Google Privacy Policy. We don't sell data or share it with anyone beyond what is described above.

How to control it

  • Stats and crash reports: Menu → ⓘ ("How to play") → "Privacy" → turn off "Send anonymous stats and crash reports".

The game immediately stops sending totals, votes and reports, and anything not yet sent is deleted from your phone. World percentages are still shown.

  • Ads: same place → "Ad settings" (in the EEA / UK / Switzerland); the advertising ID in your phone's settings;

or the "Remove ads" purchase.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and isn't made specifically for children. We collect no personal data from anyone, including children.

Sharing

The "Share" button opens your phone's own share menu. The game doesn't see or store what you send or where.

Changes to this policy

If anything changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
