Support

Found a bug, have a dilemma idea or a question? Email olyapanyuhno@gmail.com. If something isn't working, please include your phone model and what you were doing — it makes fixing it much easier.

FAQ

Do I need an account?
No. The game has no accounts.

Does the game work offline?
Yes. Only the world percentages aren't shown without internet.

Why does it say "You're among the first!" instead of percentages?
Not enough groups have voted on this dilemma yet. Percentages appear once there are enough votes.

How do I turn off stats and crash reports?
Menu → ⓘ ("How to play") → "Privacy" → turn off "Send anonymous stats and crash reports". You can keep playing as before.

How do I delete my data?
The game doesn't collect data linked to you, so there's nothing to delete on the server. Favorites and settings stay on your phone and are gone when you delete the game. More in the privacy policy.
